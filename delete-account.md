# Delete your Gravix247 account

**Last updated: 12 September 2026**

This page explains how to delete your Gravix247 account and what happens to your
data. It applies to the Gravix247 app for Android (`com.gravix247`), available on
Google Play.

There are two ways to delete your account. **Either one results in the same
deletion.**

---

## Option 1 — Delete it yourself, inside the app

1. Open the **Gravix247** app and sign in.
2. Go to **Settings → Delete Account**.
3. Follow the **two-step confirmation**.

Deletion is **immediate and permanent** once you confirm.

---

## Option 2 — Ask us to delete it, by email

**You do not need the app installed to request deletion.** If you have uninstalled
Gravix247, changed phone, cannot sign in, or are blocked by the subscription check
described below, email us and we will delete your account for you.

> **Email:** **dskj4178@gmail.com**
> **Subject line:** `Account deletion request`
> **Include:** the email address you used to sign in to Gravix247.

We will acknowledge your request within **7 days** and complete the deletion within
**30 days** of verifying it, as set out in our
[Privacy Policy](privacy-policy.html).

**You will never be prevented from having your Gravix247 account deleted.**

---

## ⚠️ If you have a paid subscription, cancel it in Google Play first

**Deleting your Gravix247 account does NOT cancel your Google Play subscription,
and Google would keep charging you for it.** They are two separate things, handled
by two separate companies — **we cannot cancel a Google Play subscription for
you.**

So that you are never billed for a service you can no longer reach, the app checks
your subscription when you tap **Delete Account**:

- **If your subscription is still set to renew** — the app will not complete the
  deletion. It will show you your next renewal date and send you to Google Play to
  cancel. **As soon as you have cancelled, you can delete your account
  immediately** — you do not have to wait for the paid period to end.
- **If your subscription is already cancelled, or you do not have one** — deletion
  proceeds straight away.
- **If we are unable to reach Google to check** — the app shows you the warning and
  lets you delete anyway.

**To cancel:** open **Google Play → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions**,
select Gravix247, and cancel.

**You will not receive a refund for the unused part of a period you have already
paid for** ([Terms of Service](terms-of-service.html), Section 7.8). If you would
rather use the time you have paid for, cancel the subscription now and delete your
account when the period ends.

**If you cannot cancel in Google Play for any reason**, use Option 2 above and we
will delete your account for you.

---

## What gets deleted from our servers

- Your profile — name, email address, mobile number, attempt year, optional subject
- All bookmarks (Prelims, Mains, Current Affairs)
- All MCQ test results and scores
- All written answers (Mains)
- Answer Library records — file metadata and Google Drive file references
- Answer writing goals
- Your device-linked identifier
- Your login account, and every sign-in session attached to it

## What gets cleared from your device

- Locally cached PDF files from the Answer Library
- Study sessions, streaks, coins, badges and Dream Box data stored on the device
- All app preferences and locally stored data

## What is **not** deleted

- **PDF files you backed up to your own Google Drive.** Those files are in your
  personal Google Drive account and remain entirely under your control. Gravix247
  does not delete them. You can remove them yourself from Google Drive at any time.
- **Your Google Play subscription.** Only you can cancel it, in Google Play. See the
  warning above.
- **Subscription and purchase records, and a hashed device reference.** These are
  retained for accounting, tax, legal and fraud-prevention purposes, as described in
  **Section 8** of our [Privacy Policy](privacy-policy.html). **They do not contain
  your name, email address or mobile number.**

---

## Is it reversible?

**No.** Account deletion is permanent and cannot be undone. If you want to use
Gravix247 again afterwards, you will need to create a new account and start again.
